# Project Roadmap

> Development roadmap for the Ayur Clinic website.

## Overview

Ayur Clinic is being developed in stages to ensure that the initial website remains focused, maintainable, and appropriate for the clinic's current requirements.

The project is currently under active development.

The roadmap is designed around a phased approach. Each phase introduces functionality based on actual requirements rather than adding unnecessary complexity at the beginning.

The current priority is to establish a high-quality informational platform with a strong technical and design foundation.

---

## Current Phase

### Phase 1 — Informational Website

The current phase focuses on creating the public-facing Ayur Clinic website.

The objective is to provide patients with clear, accessible, and useful information about the clinic and its services.

The platform includes or is planned to include:

- Homepage
- About the clinic
- Meet the team
- Consultant information
- Services
- Patient resources
- Pregnancy-related tools
- Pregnancy calculator
- Clinic location
- Parking information
- Contact information
- Privacy Policy
- Cookie Policy

This phase focuses primarily on information and patient guidance.

The website is intentionally designed to avoid unnecessary complexity or premature backend functionality.

---

## Design and User Experience

The current development phase includes continued refinement of:

- Visual hierarchy
- Typography
- Spacing
- Responsive layouts
- Navigation
- Interactive components
- Patient journeys
- Mobile experience

The design system will continue to evolve as new components and pages are developed.

The objective is to maintain a consistent and reassuring experience across the entire platform.

---

## Accessibility

Accessibility improvements are part of the ongoing development process.

Areas under review include:

- Semantic HTML
- Keyboard navigation
- Focus states
- Colour contrast
- Form accessibility
- Responsive typography
- Touch targets
- Screen-reader compatibility

The project aims to follow WCAG 2.2 AA principles where applicable.

Accessibility will continue to be reviewed throughout development and before production release.

---

## Performance

Performance optimisation will continue as the application develops.

Areas of focus include:

- Image optimisation
- Responsive image delivery
- JavaScript usage
- Component efficiency
- Third-party dependencies
- Layout stability
- Loading behaviour

Performance will be evaluated as features and content are added to ensure that the website remains responsive across different devices and network conditions.

---

## SEO and Production Preparation

Before launch, the website will require further review of:

- Page metadata
- Page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Open Graph metadata
- Structured data where appropriate
- Sitemap generation
- Robots configuration
- Canonical URLs
- Search engine indexing

Content and metadata will be reviewed based on the final approved version of the website.

---

## Phase 2 — Patient Interaction

Future development may introduce controlled patient interaction workflows.

Potential functionality may include:

- Callback requests
- Appointment enquiries
- Structured contact workflows

Any workflow involving personal information will be designed with privacy and security requirements considered before implementation.

The introduction of patient interaction features will depend on the clinic's requirements and operational processes.

---

## Planned Data Strategy

If contact or callback functionality is introduced, the platform will follow a data-minimisation approach.

The intention is to collect only the information required for the defined purpose.

Potential information may include:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number

The public website should avoid becoming a channel for uncontrolled submission of sensitive health information.

Future workflows involving sensitive information would require separate consideration and appropriate architecture.

---

## Future Backend Infrastructure

Backend functionality will only be introduced when required by a specific feature.

The planned technical direction may include:

- Supabase
- PostgreSQL
- Server-side validation
- Restricted database access
- Row Level Security where appropriate
- Controlled administrative access

The objective is to avoid adding backend infrastructure before it provides a clear benefit.

---

## Phase 3 — Platform Expansion

Future development may introduce additional digital services based on genuine clinic and patient requirements.

Potential areas may include:

- Additional patient tools
- Secure integrations
- Administrative workflows
- Payment functionality where appropriate
- Expanded digital services

These features are not part of the current informational website and will be evaluated individually before development begins.

---

## Review and Refinement

As the project progresses, the following areas will continue to be reviewed:

- Architecture
- Design system
- Accessibility
- Performance
- SEO
- Privacy
- Security
- Responsive behaviour
- Content structure

The roadmap is not intended to lock the project into unnecessary features.

It provides a structured direction while allowing technical and product decisions to evolve based on real requirements.

---

## Current Status

**Under Active Development**

The current priority is completing and refining the informational website.

Future phases will only begin when there is a clear requirement and an appropriate technical, privacy, and operational approach has been defined.

The project will continue to evolve through development, testing, review, and refinement.
